# Market Conduct and Algorithmic Trading Policy

Nordwind Energy Trading GmbH · Version 2.0 · Owner: Head of Trading, with Compliance · Approved on 2 February 2026

## 1 Purpose

1.1 This policy sets the rules of conduct for trading in wholesale energy products by Nordwind Energy Trading GmbH ("Nordwind Trading"), including trading by algorithms.

1.2 It applies to all traders, analysts, desk heads and anyone who places orders on behalf of Nordwind Trading.

## 2 Prohibited conduct

2.1 Market manipulation is strictly prohibited. Traders must not enter into transactions or place orders that give false or misleading signals as to the supply of, demand for or price of wholesale energy products, or that secure the price at an artificial level.

2.2 In particular, the following practices are prohibited: wash trades, layering and spoofing, marking the close, and placing orders without the intention to execute them.

2.3 Only the Communications department may speak to the press or publish statements about market conditions. Traders must not comment on market rumours on social media.

2.4 Trades between Nordwind group companies on EPEX SPOT are allowed to balance positions within the group.

2.5 Any employee who has concerns about possible market abuse must report them to Compliance. Reports can also be made anonymously through the group whistleblowing system, and nobody will suffer disadvantages for reporting in good faith.

## 3 Trading mandates and records

3.1 Each trader trades only within the products, markets and limits set out in his or her individual trading mandate, which is approved by the Head of Trading and Risk Management.

3.2 Trader telephone lines and Bloomberg chats are recorded. Recordings, chat logs and trade tickets are kept for five years.

## 4 Algorithmic trading

4.1 Nordwind Trading uses algorithms on the EPEX SPOT intraday market for the optimisation of its short-term position.

4.2 Every algorithm runs within pre-trade limits: a price collar of ±20% around the last traded price, a maximum order volume of 50 MW, and a maximum of 30 order messages per second.

4.3 Orders that breach a pre-trade limit are rejected automatically. The intraday desk can stop all algorithms immediately with a kill switch.

4.4 New algorithms are tested in the EPEX simulation environment before go-live and require the written approval of the Head of Trading.

4.5 The intraday desk monitors running algorithms during trading hours.

4.6 Documentation of algorithms, including strategy descriptions, parameters and test results, is kept for three years.

## 5 Review

5.1 This policy is reviewed by the Head of Trading once a year.
