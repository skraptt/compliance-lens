# Inside Information Policy

Nordwind Energy Trading GmbH · Version 3.2 · Owner: Compliance · Approved by the Management Board on 14 March 2026

## 1 Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how Nordwind Energy Trading GmbH ("Nordwind Trading") identifies, handles and publishes inside information under Regulation (EU) No 1227/2011 (REMIT).

1.2 The policy applies to all employees, temporary staff and contractors of Nordwind Trading.

1.3 Only information about facilities owned by Nordwind Trading itself needs to be published under this policy. Kraftwerk Elbufer is owned by our sister company Nordwind Erzeugung GmbH and is therefore outside the scope of this policy.

## 2 What is inside information

2.1 Inside information is information of a precise nature which has not been made public, which relates directly or indirectly to one or more wholesale energy products, and which, if it were made public, would be likely to significantly affect the prices of those products.

2.2 Compliance assesses whether information is inside information using the criteria in paragraph 2.1. As a rule of thumb, any planned or unplanned change of 100 MW or more in the available capacity of a power plant is presumed to be inside information.

## 3 Handling inside information

3.1 Employees who possess inside information must not acquire or dispose of wholesale energy products to which the information relates, for Nordwind Trading or for anyone else, and must not recommend or induce another person to do so.

3.2 Inside information may only be shared with colleagues who need it to perform their duties (need-to-know).

3.3 Employees who believe they have received inside information must inform Compliance immediately.

3.4 Compliance keeps a list of all persons who have access to inside information about the plants marketed by Nordwind Trading.

3.5 Plant operations staff sit in a separate building and have no access to trading systems.

3.6 Inside information may be shared with external advisers and banks only after they have signed a non-disclosure agreement.

## 4 Publication of inside information

4.1 Inside information is published on our company website www.nordwind-trading.example and, where possible, also on the EEX Transparency Platform.

4.2 Publication takes place within 24 hours of the information becoming known to Nordwind Trading.

4.3 Urgent Market Messages are prepared using the template in Annex A, which follows the data fields of Annex VII of the ACER Manual of Procedures on data reporting. Each message is checked by a second person from the Market Operations team before publication.

4.4 When the expected duration or the available capacity changes, the message is updated as soon as the new information is available.

4.5 Where an outage is also published on the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, this satisfies our obligation under REMIT and no separate Urgent Market Message is needed.

4.6 The EEX Transparency Platform forwards our published Urgent Market Messages to ACER.

## 5 Delayed publication

5.1 The Head of Trading may decide to delay publication of inside information if publication would harm Nordwind Trading's commercial interests.

5.2 Compliance documents each decision to delay publication, including the reasons.

## 6 Breaches

6.1 Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary measures, including dismissal.

6.2 This policy is reviewed by Compliance every two years.
