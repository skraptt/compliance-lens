# REMIT Registration and Transaction Reporting Procedure

Nordwind Energy Trading GmbH · Version 4.1 · Owner: Back Office · Approved on 20 January 2026

## 1 Registration

1.1 Nordwind Trading is registered as a market participant with the Bundesnetzagentur (ACER code A0001234N.DE). The ACER code is recorded in the static data of the trading system.

1.2 The Head of Back Office is responsible for the registration in CEREMP and for keeping it up to date.

1.3 Changes to registration data are reported to the Bundesnetzagentur at the annual review of registration data in January.

## 2 Scope of reporting

2.1 Nordwind Trading trades the following reportable products: power and natural gas spot and futures on EEX and EPEX SPOT, and OTC forwards in power and gas through brokers and bilaterally, including non-standard contracts for plant optimisation.

2.2 Only executed transactions are reported. Orders that are not executed are not reportable.

2.3 OTC trades, including non-standard contracts, are reported by Nordwind Trading through the registered reporting mechanism TradeLink RRM.

2.4 To be on the safe side, Nordwind Trading additionally reports all trades executed on EEX and EPEX SPOT through TradeLink RRM.

2.5 Intragroup trades between Nordwind companies concluded outside an organised market place are not REMIT-relevant and are not recorded for REMIT purposes.

## 3 Reporting deadlines

3.1 Standard contracts are reported by the end of the next working day (T+1).

3.2 Non-standard contracts are reported within one month of their conclusion.

## 4 Delegated reporting and controls

4.1 Trades on EEX and EPEX SPOT are also reported to ACER by the exchanges. Back Office checks every morning that the exchanges' reporting confirmations have been received.

4.2 Every working day, Back Office reconciles the trades reported through TradeLink RRM against the trade blotter of the trading system to ensure that all trades have been reported.

4.3 Rejected reports and validation errors received from TradeLink RRM are corrected and resubmitted on the same working day.

## 5 Records

5.1 Original OTC and non-standard contracts are archived in the contract management system and can be retrieved on request.

5.2 This procedure is reviewed annually by the Head of Back Office.
