# Legal-accuracy review: REMIT checklist v0.2, items added by the completeness reviewer

Reviewed file: `reviews/remit-v0.2-under-review.yaml` (not edited)
Scope: REMIT-COOP-01, COOP-02, REP-06, REP-07, REP-08, REP-09, REP-10, LNG-01, OMP-01, INS-03, INS-04, DIS-07, DIS-08, DIS-09, MAN-02, plus judgement calls STR-02.c2, REG-02.c1, REG-02.c2, COOP-01.c4
Sources: REMIT MCP (consolidated REMIT CELEX 02011R1227-20250205, IR (EU) 2026/256, DR (EU) 2026/255, ACER Guidance 6.1, ACER Q&As 30th ed., ACER Open Letters 2026). Source versions fetched 2026-10-30; sources last checked 2026-11-17. Not legal advice.

## Verdict

The new items are mostly sound. All the key dates match the primary text: 2025-01-01 for LNG, 2027-10-29 for Art 4(2) and Art 8(5) IR, 2028-04-29 for Art 4(1) and 4(3)-(7) IR, 2028-07-01 for Art 4(8) IR, 2027-10-29 for DR Arts 3-8 and 10-39, and 2024-05-07 for ACER's powers. The 18:00 CET LNG deadline, the 50 MW and 600 GWh hydrogen thresholds, the annual hydrogen deadline (end of the following January) and the five-year retention periods are also correct. Art 13a/13b content is broadly correct. **The item cannot ship as it stands, because four `basis: law` checks go beyond what the law requires and would produce false GAP findings:**

- COOP-01.c4 (preservation on receipt of a request)
- COOP-02.c1 (store original contracts)
- REP-09.c3 ("written" confirmation)
- REP-07.c4 (rejection-handling procedure)

Two of the judgement-call checks (REG-02.c2 and STR-02.c2) also need downgrading. Beyond that, there are two material timing or completeness problems in REP-09 and one misstatement of Art 3(4)(a) in INS-03.

## Judgement calls

1. **REMIT-STR-02.c2 ("surveillance staff do not report to the trading function"): change to `guidance`.** Art 15(3)(b) requires arrangements that keep surveillance staff free of conflicts of interest and acting independently. It does not prescribe any reporting line. That legal duty is already captured by c3 (`law`). The structural expectation comes from ACER Guidance 9.4.3.1 (governance, separation from commercial interests), which is written for PPATs. For PPETs, ACER stresses proportionality (9.4.1 para (465), 9.4.2 para (471)).
   Proposed: `- {id: c2, basis: guidance, text: "States that surveillance staff do not report to, and cannot be overruled by, the trading or commercial function."}`
   Sources: Art 15(3) REMIT …#art_15; ACER Guidance 9.4.3.1 (…#page=124).

2. **REMIT-REG-02.c1 (list of trigger events): split it.** Art 9(5) requires *any* change to registration-form information to be communicated promptly. A procedure that commits to this is legally grounded. A *list* of specific trigger events is a means of implementation, and the law prescribes no such list.
   Proposed: `- {id: c1, basis: law, text: "Requires any change to the information in the registration form (e.g. management, ownership, publication of inside information) to be communicated to the NRA."}` and `- {id: c1b, basis: good_practice, text: "Lists the internal events that trigger a registration update."}`
   Source: Art 9(5) REMIT …#art_9; ACER Guidance 8.9 paras (395)-(396).

3. **REMIT-REG-02.c2 (a time limit for notifying changes): change to `good_practice`, and keep the "promptly" duty as law.** The law says "promptly" and sets no numeric deadline. ACER Guidance 8.9 adds none, and treats a registration as incomplete if changes are not communicated promptly. A procedure that says "promptly" with no internal day count is not in breach.
   Proposed: `- {id: c2, basis: law, text: "Requires changes to be notified to the NRA promptly."}` and `- {id: c2b, basis: good_practice, text: "Sets an internal time limit (e.g. number of working days) for notifying registration changes."}`
   Source: Art 9(5) REMIT; ACER Guidance 8.9 (…#page=111).

4. **REMIT-COOP-01.c4 (preserve recordings, chats and e-mails on receipt of an inspection or request): change to `good_practice`.** Art 13(2)(d) only lets NRAs *require existing* records. Art 13a and 13b give ACER powers to inspect and request, and make the addressee responsible for the accuracy of what it supplies. None of them imposes a REMIT duty to preserve, or a "legal hold" procedure. The ACER Manual for new market participants (section 6) recommends keeping transaction communications for at least five years. That is non-binding, so at most a separate `guidance` retention check could be added. Destroying evidence after a request may breach national law, but that is outside the REMIT checklist.
   Sources: Art 13(2)(d), 13a, 13b REMIT; ACER Manual 2026 section 6.

## Items confirmed correct (no change needed beyond the above)

- **REMIT-REP-06**: Correct throughout. Art 8(1) IR (OMP reports; MPs shall not report that data), 8(3) (access to reported data and validation results on request), 8(4) (off-OMP reporting by MP) and 8(5) (MPs give missing info to the OMP no later than the Art 10 deadline, from 2027-10-29 under Art 17(3)) are all stated accurately. The check bases are right.
- **REMIT-DIS-07**: Art 4(4) (transparency publication is effective but not necessarily timely and public disclosure) and Art 4(6) (transparency rules continue to apply) are stated correctly. c1 is properly `law`, because Art 4(1) requires disclosure through IIPs.
- **REMIT-DIS-08**: Art 4(7) is stated correctly: a right to delay for classified critical-infrastructure information, with a check that is properly `good_practice`. Note that REMIT still refers to Directive 2008/114/EC.
- **Dates and thresholds verified as correct**: LNG start 2025-01-01 and the 18:00 CET deadline (Art 7c(1)); ACER powers from 2024-05-07; Art 4(2) IR from 2027-10-29; Art 4(1) and 4(3)-(7) IR from 2028-04-29; Art 4(8) IR from 2028-07-01, annual, by the end of January; 50 MW and 600 GWh hydrogen exemptions; Art 5(3) and 4(11) five-year retention; OMP five-year retention (8(3)); DR 2026/255 Arts 3-8 and 10-39 from 2027-10-29 (Art 40).
- **Article 13a/13b substance**: Correct as stated in COOP-01. Unannounced inspections are allowed (13a(2)), inspections are ordered by ACER decision (13a(6)), and replies must be "complete, correct and not misleading" (13b(3)). - **Article 2(1)(ca) and the front-running framing in INS-04**: Correct, apart from the c1 wording.
- **Addressees**: Correct for REP-06, REP-08, REP-10, LNG-01, INS-03, INS-04, DIS-07, DIS-08 and MAN-02.
